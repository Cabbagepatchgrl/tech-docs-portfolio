    Standard Operating Procedure: Executive Support Operations

    2. Schedule, Availability & Duty Hours

    • Regular Work Schedule: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM
    • General Availability: Fully reachable via email, Teams, and phone between 8:00 AM and 4:30 PM.
    • Regular Break Window: 12:30 PM or 1:00 PM (flexible depending on operational duties and task coverage).
    • Leave & Work-From-Home (WFH) Protocol:
    • Send advance email notification directly to Leadership, the Operations Manager, and peer administrative staff.
    • Add calendar reminders and Out of Office (OOO) holds on your primary Outlook calendar for all approved away days.

    3. Daily Operational Flow

    Phase 1: Morning Start-Up & Calendar Triage (8:00 AM – 9:00 AM)

    1. System & Outlook Audit: Log in and review incoming Outlook requests, Teams messages, and emails from both internal and external stakeholders.
    2. Prioritize Leadership Requests: Accept or decline meeting invitations promptly. Bring immediate awareness to Leadership regarding high-priority or sensitive external meeting requests.
    3. Handle Non-Outlook Meeting Requests: For requests sent via email, chat, or verbal communication from internal department staff, direct the requester to send a formal Outlook invite or manually create the Outlook calendar event.
    4. Calendar Standard Enforcement: Verify that every meeting request generated within the Department contains a physical conference room reservation, a Microsoft Teams link, or both.

    Phase 2: Mid-Morning Operations & Daily Support (9:00 AM – 12:30 PM)

    1. Visitor Management & Front Office Readiness:
    2. Maintain a clean, professional, and welcoming environment in the front office area.
    3. Coordinate escort requests and visitor passes in advance. Confirm security clearance and building access before guest arrival.
    4. Greet and escort visitors between locations as needed.
    5. Weekly Check-Ins & Alignments:
    6. Leadership Syncs: Schedule and maintain recurring weekly check-ins with the Director and Deputy Director. Advance-review their calendars to flag/resolve scheduling conflicts, confirm stakeholder meetings, verify conference rooms, and update agendas.
    7. Operations Syncs: Attend weekly alignment meetings with Leadership and the Operations Manager to review operations updates, track action items, and align on team priorities.
    8. Meeting & Presentation Support:
    9. All-Hands Meetings: Assist Leadership with editing and formatting presentation slides for accuracy and branding compliance. Coordinate All-Hands agendas in advance.
    10. Monthly Meetings: Collaborate with the Operations Manager to plan, prepare materials for, and facilitate monthly team meetings.

    Phase 3: Afternoon Administration & Systems Tracking (1:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

    1. Travel Coordination & Expense Management:
    2. Smartsheet Tracking: Maintain and update official Smartsheets tracking logs for Leadership travel, including trip details, lodging, transportation, and pre-approvals.
    3. CAR Systems: Submit car entry requests for DOE trips, conferences, or paid events in coordination with the CAR Systems Point of Contact (POC).
    4. Concur System: Manage all travel bookings, voucher submissions, reimbursements, and cancellations for Leadership using Concur.
    5. SharePoint & Document Management:
    6. Organize, name, and upload official files to SharePoint while maintaining strict version control.
    7. Bi-Weekly ATAAPS Time & Attendance Support:
    8. Submit or review ATAAPS timesheets for federal staff every other week.
    9. Confirm time charges and leave entries with staff before submission deadlines.
    10. Update staff schedule types in ATAAPS based on approved schedule changes prior to the start of the pay period.

    Phase 4: End-of-Day Wrap-Up & Ad-Hoc Duties (4:00 PM – 4:30 PM)

    1. Daily Reconcile: Perform a final review of next-day executive calendars to ensure all conference rooms and virtual links are locked in.
    2. Additional & Backup Support: Execute additional operational tasks assigned by Leadership or Supervisors, and provide administrative backup support for peer admin staff as needed.

    4. Verification & Output Standards

    • Calendar Accuracy: Executive calendars reflect zero unresolved double-bookings, and all internal invitations contain room or virtual link details.
    • Travel & Compliance: Concur vouchers and CAR requests are submitted within required agency timelines with complete Smartsheet documentation.
    • Operational Readiness: ATAAPS submissions are finalized prior to bi-weekly deadlines, and visitor access is secured prior to arrival.

    Other Duties Take on additional administrative or operational tasks as assigned. Provide back-up support to other admin staff as needed.
